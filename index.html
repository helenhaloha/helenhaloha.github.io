Introduction

Mapo Tofu is a classic Sichuan dish made with soft tofu, ground meat, chili bean paste, and Sichuan peppercorns. It is famous for its bold spicy, numbing, and savory flavors, with tender tofu that absorbs the rich sauce.

Ingredient

  • Soft tofu, 1 block
  • Ground meat, 100 g
  • Chili bean paste, 1 tbsp
  • Peppercorns, 1 tsp
  • Minced ginger & garlic, 1tsp
  • Broth or water, 200 ml
  • Cornstarch, 1tsp (mix with water)

Steps

  1. Cut tofu into cubes and boil briefly, set aside.
  2. Heat oil, fry peppercorns until fragrant, remove them.
  3. Add ginger and garlic, stir-fry quickly.
  4. Add ground meat, cook until browned.
  5. Stir in chili bean paste, fry until red oil comes out.
  6. Pour in broth, bring to a boil.
  7. Add tofu, simmer gently for a few minutes.
  8. Thicken with cornstarch slurry.
  9. Ready to serve!
